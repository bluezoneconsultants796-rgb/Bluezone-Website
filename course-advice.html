Course advice: pick a subject that fits your career and Pakistani rules

Check three things before you apply: entry requirements, job outcomes and whether the degree counts for licensing at home.

Entry requirements: the 14 versus 16-year question

Pakistani bachelor’s degrees come in 2-year (BA, BSc) and 4-year (BS, BSc Hons) forms. Many universities abroad treat a 4-year degree as the equal of a bachelor’s abroad, while a 2-year degree may need a bridging year. Rules differ by university, so read each course page and email admissions if the page is silent.

Watch out: For medicine in particular, do not rely on an agent’s statement that a degree is “recognised”. Ask for the regulator’s written confirmation for your exact university and programme. Our China guide explains the process for Chinese MBBS.

How to choose a course

  1. Write the job title you wantSearch live job adverts and note the required degree, skills and employer location.
  2. List five courses that lead thereCompare the modules, placement options and accreditation.
  3. Ask for graduate outcomesUniversities publish employment and salary data for courses. Ask for yours.
  4. Check the length and costA one-year master’s in the UK or Ireland costs much less than a two-year degree elsewhere, but the course load is heavier.
  5. Check a backup coursePick one safer course in case your first choice has strict entry or a visa limit.

Red flags in a course

  • The provider is not on the official register for your destination
  • The course is not accredited by the body your career needs
  • The marketing promises a job or a visa
  • Teaching hours are very low, with most delivery online
  • The fee is unusually low with no clear explanation
  • You cannot find an alumni outcome or graduate employment data

Frequently asked questions

Can I do a master’s abroad with a 14-year degree?

Sometimes. Some universities accept a 2-year bachelor’s plus a 2-year master’s, and others ask for a bridging or postgraduate diploma. Always email admissions with your transcripts to ask.

Which subjects are best for jobs?

Demand changes. Technology, healthcare, engineering and finance are strong in many markets, and placements and visa-friendly employers matter more than the label. Use live job data.

Is a one-year UK master’s respected?

Yes, widely. It is a full master’s degree with a heavier workload. Check whether your target employer or profession requires a two-year degree.

Will a foreign degree be recognised in Pakistan?

For most jobs, yes. For regulated professions you need the regulator’s approval, so check first.

Can I switch course after I arrive?

Usually only with approval and sometimes with a new visa. Ask the international office before you change.
