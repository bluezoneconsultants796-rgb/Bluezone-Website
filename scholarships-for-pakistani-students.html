Scholarships for Pakistani students: calendar and how to win

How to prepare a winning application

  1. Start the year beforeStrong applications need references, essays and an English score, all of which take weeks.
  2. Read the selection criteriaEach award wants leadership, impact or research potential. Write evidence against each criterion.
  3. Use specific examplesName the project, the number of people, the result and what you learned.
  4. Get two strong referencesAsk people who know your work well and give them a one-page brief.
  5. Use a clear career planShow how the course feeds a concrete role or research question in Pakistan or beyond.
  6. Apply to more than oneAdd university scholarships and at least one other major award.

HEC and Pakistani routes to check

  • HEC overseas scholarships and nominations for CSC, Commonwealth, Stipendium Hungaricum and similar programmes run through HEC. Watch the HEC portal for live calls.
  • USEFP runs the Fulbright programme and EducationUSA advising for Pakistan.
  • Provincial and federal funds change over time. Ask your university’s scholarship office for any current options.
  • Interest-free education loans for study at top-ranked universities have been offered by Pakistani government schemes in recent years. Check eligibility and current status with the scheme or HEC before you plan around it. See education loans.

Mistakes that cost scholarships

  • Missing the deadline by a day, or ignoring the time zone
  • Using one generic essay for every award
  • Weak references on plain paper without a clear academic or employer link
  • Ignoring eligibility rules such as age limits, work experience or previous study abroad
  • Paying a “scholarship consultant” for a free application
Watch out: Government scholarships do not charge application fees. If someone asks you to pay for a Chevening, Fulbright or CSC place, it is a scam.

Frequently asked questions

When does Chevening close?

For the 2027/28 awards, applications close on 6 October 2026 at 11:00 UTC.

Which scholarship is easiest to get?

None is easy. Competition is high for fully funded awards. Part-tuition scholarships from universities are more common and can stack with other funds.

Do I need IELTS for a scholarship?

Most awards require an English score before you start the course, and some at the application stage. Check the page for your scholarship.

Can I get a scholarship for a bachelor’s degree?

Yes, in some countries such as Türkiye, China and Hungary, and at many universities. Fully funded bachelor’s awards are rarer in the UK and USA.

Where do I find live deadlines?

On each scholarship’s official page and the HEC portal. Do not rely on social media posts.
