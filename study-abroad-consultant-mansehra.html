Mansehra, and why a local office matters

Mansehra is a district headquarters on the Karakoram Highway and the gateway to the Kaghan Valley, Naran and Lake Saiful Muluk. Families here are used to long journeys — a consultancy should not add another one.

The town has deep roots: the Ashoka rock edicts near Mansehra, carved in the 3rd century BCE, are among the oldest inscriptions in the region. Hazara University at Dhodial gives local students a first taste of campus life; many now look further abroad for a degree.

Because our office is above the Passport Office, students can start a passport question and a study-abroad question on the same trip.
