Haripur, the first stop into Hazara

Haripur was founded in 1822 by Hari Singh Nalwa, a general of the Sikh Empire. Today it is where the Hazara region begins for travelers coming from Islamabad, with the Karakoram Highway and the Hazara Expressway running through it.

The district is known for Tarbela Dam on the Indus, Khanpur Dam and the Hattar industrial estate. Engineering, energy and manufacturing families here often have a practical question: which degree abroad leads somewhere useful?

The University of Haripur gives local students a home campus. For those wanting a degree abroad, the route runs through the same steps as anywhere else — assessment, shortlist, documents, application.

Haripur FAQs

Does Bluezone have an office in Haripur?

No. Our offices are in Mansehra, Abbottabad and Perth (WA). Abbottabad is the nearest to Haripur.

Where should a Haripur student go?

To our Abbottabad office, near Old Police Chowki, Supply. You can also start with the contact form on our website before making the trip.

Can I begin without traveling?

Yes. Send your academic details and preferred country through the contact form; we reply within one working day.

What should I bring to the first visit?

Your latest transcripts and certificates, your passport if you have one, and a rough budget. We give you a per-university checklist after shortlisting.

Which countries do you guide for?

China, Italy, Lithuania, the United Kingdom, France and Cyprus, plus other European destinations through our counseling desk.

Do you promise admission?

No. Nobody honestly can. We prepare an accurate application and track it to a decision.
