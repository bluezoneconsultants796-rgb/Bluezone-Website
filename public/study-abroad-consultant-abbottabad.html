Abbottabad: a city built around education

Founded in 1853 and named after Major James Abbott, Abbottabad is home to the Pakistan Military Academy at Kakul, Ayub Medical College and campuses such as COMSATS University Islamabad’s Abbottabad campus and Abbottabad University of Science & Technology.

The Galiyat hills — Thandiani, Nathia Gali and Ayubia National Park — sit just above the city. Students who grow up here are comfortable with cool climates, which is a real consideration when choosing between, say, Lithuania and Cyprus.

Medical and engineering aspirants are common in Abbottabad, so many first conversations with us are about MBBS routes and how recognition depends on the university’s current listing and the rules in force when you return.
