Study in France: Campus France, visa, fees, universities and scholarships for Pakistani students

Why Pakistani students choose France

  • Respected public universities and grandes écoles with moderate tuition compared with the UK or USA.
  • Many English-taught master’s and MBA programmes, plus French-taught degrees at low public fees.
  • Study work allowance of 964 hours a year and housing aid for students in many cities.
  • Career access to Paris and Europe in aerospace, luxury, engineering, AI, business and hospitality.

Step by step: from offer to visa

  1. Create a Campus France (Études en France) filePakistan uses the Campus France procedure. Apply to programmes and complete the file with your documents.
  2. Attend the Campus France interviewOfficers in Karachi, Lahore or Islamabad assess your study plan and motivation.
  3. Receive acceptance and book the visaApply on the France-Visas portal, pay the fee and book your appointment.
  4. Show funds and housingBank statements or sponsor letters showing about €615 per month, plus accommodation proof.
  5. Validate on arrivalValidate your VLS-TS online with OFII after you arrive. A fee applies.
Campus France interviews check that your plan makes sense: why this course, why France, and how it links to your past study. Rehearse your answers and keep your funds documents clean. The monthly funds figure has been reported higher in some updates, so confirm the current amount before you apply.

Work rights, English and after graduation

  • The VLS-TS visa lets students work up to 964 hours per year.
  • Eligible master’s graduates can apply for a temporary residence permit of up to 12 months to look for work. Check the current rule.
  • French-taught courses need DELF or TCF. English-taught courses usually accept IELTS 6.0 to 6.5.

Scholarships and funding

  • Eiffel Excellence Scholarship for master’s and PhD.
  • Campus France scholarship catalogue and university awards.
  • Erasmus Mundus joint master’s with full funding.
  • CAF housing aid can reduce rent once you live in France.

Frequently asked questions

Is Campus France compulsory for Pakistani students?

Yes, for the long-stay student visa you follow the Campus France (Études en France) procedure, including an interview.

How much money do I need?

The commonly quoted minimum is €615 per month. Confirm the latest figure before applying.

Can I study in France in English?

Yes. Many master’s, MBA and some bachelor’s courses are taught in English.

Can I work in France as a student?

Yes, up to 964 hours a year on the student visa.

How early should I start?

Start 8 to 10 months before your intake because of Campus France deadlines.
