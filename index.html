Ask a counsellor
Is the first counselling session free?

Yes. Book a session at any office or online and we will map out countries, universities and budget with you.

Which countries can you help me with?

Australia, UK, Canada, USA, New Zealand, Ireland, Malaysia and China through our partner office in Guangxi.

How early should I start?

Start 9 to 12 months before your intake. That leaves time for tests, documents, offers and your visa.

Do you help with scholarships?

Yes. We check which scholarships fit your grades and country, then help you prepare the application.

Which documents do I need first?

Passport, academic transcripts and certificates, an English test result if you have one, and your CV.

Can you help after I get an offer?

Yes. We support with acceptance, visa file checks, money transfer, accommodation and pre-departure advice.

Why study in the UK

  • Globally respected universities with a long record of teaching and research.
  • Programmes from foundation and bachelor’s to doctorate level.
  • Strong industry links, placements and internships built into many degrees.
  • Shorter master’s degrees (often one year) can lower total cost.
  • The Graduate Route lets eligible graduates stay and work after study. Check the current length before you apply.

Not sure where to start?

Tell us your grades, budget and subject. We shortlist universities and explain what each intake needs.

Student visa basics

  • Apply up to three months before your course starts.
  • You need a Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS).
  • Show funds for tuition and living costs.
  • Valid passport and passport-size photo.
  • TB test and ATAS clearance if your course or country requires it.

FAQs

How much does it cost to study in the UK?

Tuition is usually 10,000 to 20,000 GBP per year for bachelor’s and master’s, plus living costs. Use the estimate tool above.

Can I work while studying?

Student visas allow limited part-time work during term. Your visa letter states the exact hours.

How do I stay after graduation?

Most students use the Graduate Route, then move to a skilled worker route if they find a sponsoring employer.

Which intake should I choose?

September is the main intake with the widest course choice. January and later intakes have fewer courses.
