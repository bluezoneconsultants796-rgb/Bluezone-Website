Education loan: options, documents and the currency risk

Borrow only after you know the full cost in rupees and have stress-tested a weaker rupee. Compare bank loans, interest-free schemes and scholarships.

Documents banks and schemes usually ask for

  • Offer letter and fee schedule
  • CNIC, passport and family details
  • Guarantor’s CNIC, income proof, bank statements and NTN
  • Collateral papers where required, such as property title
  • Academic documents and test results
  • Estimated cost sheet with tuition, living costs and travel
  • Visa evidence at disbursement stage, if the lender asks

Ask each lender for its written schedule of fees, mark-up type, repayment start date and what happens if your visa is refused or you leave the course.

Risks to plan for

  • Currency. If the rupee weakens after you borrow, costs in pounds, dollars or dirhams rise in rupees.
  • Rate type. A floating rate can rise during repayment.
  • Income gap. You may begin repayment before your first job. Ask about a grace period.
  • Visa refusal or dropout. Check what happens to fees you have already paid.
  • Over-borrowing. Test the payback years from the return check.
Watch out: A loan sanction letter is not proof of funds in every country. Check each visa’s funds rule before you rely on borrowed money.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get an interest-free loan to study abroad?

Pakistani government and endowment schemes have offered interest-free overseas study loans with strict eligibility, often tied to the university’s ranking. Programmes change, so check current status and rules before you plan around one.

Do banks in Pakistan give education loans for abroad?

Several banks offer education financing, usually with collateral or a guarantor. Terms, mark-up and eligibility vary. Compare written offers from at least two lenders.

Is a loan accepted as proof of funds?

It depends on the destination. Some visa rules accept an education loan letter from a recognised lender, others want funds held in an account. Check the official visa page.

What if the rupee falls after I borrow?

Your costs in foreign currency rise in rupees while your loan amount is fixed in rupees. Keep a buffer and use the calculator above with a weaker rupee.

Should I borrow the full amount?

Borrow what you need after scholarships and savings, and keep repayments below what you can realistically earn.
