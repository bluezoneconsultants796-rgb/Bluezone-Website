Study in Dubai and the UAE: visa, universities, costs and Golden Visa for Pakistani students

Why Pakistani students choose Dubai and the UAE

  • A short flight from Pakistan with a large Pakistani community and halal food everywhere.
  • International branch campuses (UK, Australian, US and Indian universities) in Dubai and Abu Dhabi.
  • No personal income tax and a safe, modern city.
  • Golden Visa options for outstanding students and graduates.

Step by step: from offer to visa

  1. Choose a licensed universityCheck the university is accredited by KHDA (Dubai) or the relevant authority.
  2. Get admission and pay depositThe university issues your offer and handles the visa.
  3. Entry permitThe university files your student entry permit.
  4. Arrive and complete medical testDo the medical, biometrics and Emirates ID.
  5. Receive your residence visaUsually valid for one year, renewed each year.
Your university sponsors the student visa. Confirm exactly what the university charges for the visa, deposits and insurance, and never pay anyone outside the university’s official account.

Work rights, English and after graduation

  • Students may work part-time with a permit. Hours are often quoted at 15 per week during term. Confirm with your university.
  • Outstanding students and graduates may qualify for the Golden Visa.
  • English is the language of instruction at most branch campuses.

Scholarships and funding

  • University merit scholarships.
  • Golden Visa for outstanding students.
  • Government scholarships in some cases.

Frequently asked questions

How does the UAE student visa work?

Your university sponsors it. You get an entry permit, then complete a medical test and Emirates ID.

How much does it cost?

Typical packages quoted are AED 5,500 to 8,500. Confirm with your university.

Can I work?

With a permit, part-time. Confirm hours.

Is the Golden Visa available?

For outstanding students and graduates meeting the conditions.

Which intakes?

September and January are main intakes.
