Study in Malaysia: Student Pass, costs, universities and twinning for Pakistani students

Why Pakistani students choose Malaysia

  • Globally recognised degrees at a much lower cost than the UK, USA or Australia.
  • English-taught programmes, and branch campuses of Monash, Nottingham, Heriot-Watt and others.
  • Twinning routes such as 2+1 and 3+0 let you finish a foreign degree with less time abroad.
  • Muslim-friendly, low-cost cities such as Kuala Lumpur, with halal food and prayer facilities widely available.

Step by step: from offer to visa

  1. Choose a university and coursePublic universities and private colleges both host international students.
  2. Accept the offer and pay the feeThe university applies to EMGS for you.
  3. Receive your eVALThe electronic Visa Approval Letter must arrive before you travel.
  4. Travel, medical and Student PassArrive with the eVAL and any Single Entry Visa, then complete the medical screening.
  5. Get your pass endorsedThe Student Pass and i-Kad are issued after arrival.
Common delays: missing documents, unclear passport copies, late payment of EMGS fees and incomplete medical screening. Always apply through the university, since EMGS does not accept individual applications.

Work rights, English and after graduation

  • Work rights are very limited. Language-centre students cannot work at all, and other students need permission and may only work in certain sectors during breaks.
  • Do not plan to fund your studies through part-time work in Malaysia.
  • There is no automatic post-study work visa like the UK or Australia. Graduates usually need an employer to sponsor a work pass.
  • English: many courses accept IELTS 5.5 to 6.0, and some accept MUET or English-medium proof. Confirm with the university.

Scholarships and funding

  • University merit scholarships, often 10 to 50 percent of tuition.
  • Malaysian International Scholarship for eligible postgraduate applicants.
  • Research assistantships at public universities.
  • Confirm dates and eligibility with each institution.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a Malaysia student visa cost?

The Student Pass itself is RM 60 per year, but the EMGS processing fee, eVAL, medical screening, insurance and i-Kad add up to roughly RM 1,500 to RM 3,500 in the first year, depending on your institution.

Do Pakistani students need a visa?

Yes. The university applies for your Student Pass through EMGS, and you may also need a Single Entry Visa before travel.

Can I work in Malaysia while studying?

Only in limited cases with permission, and language-centre students cannot work at all. Plan your budget without work income.

What is twinning?

A programme where you spend part of the degree in Malaysia and the rest at a partner university abroad, for example 2+1, or take the full foreign degree on a branch campus.

How long does it take?

Typically 4 to 8 weeks for the eVAL. Apply as soon as you accept your offer.

When are the intakes?

Most universities offer January or February, May and September. Some have rolling intakes.
