Study in the USA: F-1 visa, costs, universities and OPT for Pakistani students

A researched guide with 2026 figures from official and industry sources. Last verified 29 September 2026.

Why Pakistani students choose the USA

  • The widest choice of colleges and universities, from state schools to the Ivy League.
  • Flexible degrees, strong research and funded master’s and PhD places with assistantships.
  • OPT lets graduates work in their field, and STEM degrees can add 24 months.
  • Large Pakistani student and professional networks in Texas, New York, California and Illinois.

Step by step: from offer to visa

  1. Apply and get admittedApply through each university or the Common App, with tests if required.
  2. Receive your I-20Submit proof of funds to the school and receive the Form I-20.
  3. Pay the SEVIS feePay $350 at fmjfee.com before the interview.
  4. Complete DS-160 and book the interviewPay the $185 fee and follow the current Embassy instructions.
  5. Attend the visa interviewShow your I-20, funds, study plan and ties to Pakistan.
Important 2026 change: DHS published a final rule on 17 July 2026 ending open-ended Duration of Status for F-1 students. From 15 September 2026, admission is for a fixed period, generally up to four years, with a shorter departure window. The rule faces legal challenges, so confirm with your school’s DSO.

Work rights, English and after graduation

  • On-campus work is usually limited to 20 hours per week in term time.
  • CPT allows paid work linked to your course after the first academic year in most programmes.
  • OPT: 12 months after graduation. STEM OPT adds 24 months for eligible degrees and E-Verify employers.
  • H-1B and other work visas are separate and competitive. Rules change often, so plan with a school advisor.

Scholarships and funding

  • University merit awards, especially at private schools.
  • Research and teaching assistantships for many master’s and PhD places.
  • Fulbright programme for Pakistani graduate students.
  • Test rules: SAT or ACT are optional at many schools. GRE or GMAT depend on programme.

Frequently asked questions

How much does an F-1 visa cost?

Most students pay the $350 SEVIS fee and the $185 visa application fee. A $250 Visa Integrity Fee is also being collected at some posts, so check current instructions.

What is the new fixed admission rule?

From 15 September 2026, F-1 students are admitted for a fixed period tied to their programme, generally capped at four years. Extensions go through USCIS, and the post-study departure window shortens to 30 days for new admissions.

Can I work in the USA as a student?

On campus up to 20 hours per week during term. Off-campus work is only through CPT or OPT and needs authorisation.

How long is OPT?

12 months after your degree. STEM graduates at E-Verify employers can apply for another 24 months.

Do I need IELTS or TOEFL?

Most schools ask for one, and many accept Duolingo or similar. Requirements differ by university, so check your programme page.

When should I apply?

Start 10 to 14 months before Fall intake. Interview dates can be limited, so book early.
