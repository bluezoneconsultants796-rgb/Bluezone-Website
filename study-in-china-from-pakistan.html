Study in China from Pakistan

Who this guide is for

This page is for Pakistani students considering university study in China — including MBBS/Medicine, Engineering, Business and IT programs — and who want to understand how Bluezone's guidance actually works before starting an application.

Programs

MBBS/Medicine, Engineering, Business and IT are the program areas Bluezone most commonly supports for China. For a medical degree, always verify PMDC recognition of the specific university directly from PMDC's official records before committing — Bluezone checks this as part of shortlisting, but the authoritative source is PMDC itself.

Costs and scholarships

Tuition and living costs vary widely by city and university, and by whether a scholarship (e.g. a Chinese Government Scholarship or a university-specific award) applies. Because these figures change yearly, Bluezone does not publish a single fixed number here — every student receives a cost breakdown, sourced to the university's own published fee schedule, during shortlisting.

Our China partner office

Guangxi Future Link, based in Guangxi, China, is Bluezone's partner office supporting students once they are in China — from arrival logistics to staying in touch with a local point of contact during the program.

China study FAQs

Does Bluezone have a presence in China?

Yes — a partner office, Guangxi Future Link, in Guangxi, China, supports students on the ground.

Can I study MBBS in China through Bluezone?

MBBS/Medicine is one of the program areas we support. Requirements, recognition status and costs are verified per university rather than quoted as a single fixed figure.

Does Bluezone guarantee admission or a scholarship in China?

No. We prepare a complete, accurate application and track it to a decision. Admission and scholarship decisions are made by the university and the relevant scholarship body.

What should I verify before choosing a Chinese university?

Its accreditation, and — for medical programs — PMDC recognition, checked directly against official sources.

Is the first consultation free?

Yes, and it carries no obligation.
