Perth and the Pakistan–WA connection

Perth sits on the Swan River, home to Kings Park and the port city of Fremantle, and is one of the most isolated major cities in the world. Distances here shape decisions, so online first conversations are common.

Western Australia has its own university landscape — the University of Western Australia, Curtin University, Murdoch University, Edith Cowan University and the University of Notre Dame Australia. Our published destination guides cover China, Italy, Lithuania, the UK, France and Cyprus.

Perth is on Australian Western Standard Time, three hours ahead of Pakistan. If your family is in Mansehra or Abbottabad, that offset matters when scheduling calls.
