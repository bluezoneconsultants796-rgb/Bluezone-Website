How to choose a study abroad consultant in Mansehra

Before you pay anyone, check these points. They protect you from the mistakes we see most often.

  1. Written feesGet every fee and what it covers in writing before you pay.
  2. No visa guaranteesNobody can promise a visa. Walk away from anyone who does.
  3. Direct university applicationsAsk which universities they apply to and whether you receive the offer letter in your own name.
  4. Your documents stay yoursKeep your own copies and your own application accounts.
  5. Pay universities directlyTuition should go to the university’s official account, with receipts. See our money transfer guide.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the best study abroad consultants in Mansehra?

Judge a consultant by what they put in writing, not by promises. Ask for the full fee list, which universities they can apply to directly, and how they handle visa refusals. BlueZone Consultants gives written costs and never promises a visa.

Is counselling free?

Counselling with us is free. If a service fee applies later, ask for it in writing before you pay.

Which countries can I apply to from Mansehra?

We guide students to the UK, Australia, Canada, USA, New Zealand, Ireland, Malaysia, China, Dubai and UAE, Italy, France, South Korea, Cyprus and Lithuania.

Can I get help with IELTS in Mansehra?

Yes. See the IELTS pages below for preparation plans and how to book your test, including the test city for students from Mansehra and Abbottabad.

Can I be counselled online?

Yes. Students anywhere in KPK can book an online session and share documents securely, then visit our office only if they want to.
