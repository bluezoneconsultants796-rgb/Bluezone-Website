What a study abroad consultant does

A study abroad consultant is not a shortcut around university or embassy decisions. In practical terms, the job has five parts: helping a student pick a realistic shortlist of universities and countries, preparing a complete and accurate application, checking which scholarships a student genuinely qualifies for before any fee is paid, verifying documents before submission, and giving educational guidance on the visa process. Anyone who tells you they can guarantee an admission, a scholarship or a visa outcome is describing something no consultant, including Bluezone, can control — those decisions sit with universities and embassies.

How to evaluate a consultancy before you commit

Pakistan-wide FAQs

What does a study abroad consultant actually do?

A study abroad consultant helps a student shortlist realistic universities and countries, prepares and files the application, screens which scholarships the student genuinely qualifies for, checks documents before submission, and gives educational guidance on the visa process. A consultant does not issue visas or admission decisions — those are made by universities and embassies.

How should I evaluate a study abroad consultancy in Pakistan?

Ask for a physical office address you can visit, ask which universities and destinations they actually support, ask them to show the official source for any scholarship or visa claim, and be cautious of anyone who guarantees an outcome no consultant can control.

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. The first consultation carries no obligation.
