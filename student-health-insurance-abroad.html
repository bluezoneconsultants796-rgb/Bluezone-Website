Health insurance: what you must buy for each country

Health cover is a visa condition in several countries. Know what is included, what is not and how to claim.

Costs are guides. Always read the policy and confirm the current price with the official body or the university.

What to check in a policy

  • Starts on arrival and covers the whole course plus any bridging period
  • Covers hospital, GP, emergency and mental health care
  • Pre-existing conditions and how they are treated
  • Repatriation, and what happens if a family member needs to visit
  • Deductible, co-pay and claim steps
  • Whether dental, optical and prescriptions are covered

How to claim and where to go

  • Keep your insurance card and policy number in your phone and wallet.
  • Register with a local GP or the university health service in week one.
  • For emergencies use the national emergency number first. Claim later using receipts.
  • Mental health counselling is usually free through the university, so use it early.

Frequently asked questions

Is the UK health surcharge refundable?

If your visa is refused, the surcharge is refunded. Check the GOV.UK page for the current rules.

Do I need OSHC in Australia?

Yes. Student visa holders must keep OSHC for the whole stay.

Can I use my Pakistani insurance abroad?

Usually not as a replacement for the required cover. Check the visa condition for your destination.

What does health insurance not cover?

Often dental, optical and some pre-existing conditions. Read the policy exclusions.

What if I need mental health support?

Use the university’s free counselling service and your insurance’s helpline. Early contact makes a difference.
