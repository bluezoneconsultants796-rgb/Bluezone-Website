Questions from students in Mansehra and KPK

Can I get help with Ireland admission in Mansehra or Abbottabad?

Yes. BlueZone Consultants counsels students from Mansehra, Abbottabad, Hazara and the rest of KPK, at our offices or online. We help with course choice, applications, documents and the visa file.

Do I have to travel to Islamabad for Ireland study plans?

Counselling, shortlisting, application and document checks can be done locally or online. Some steps, such as a visa application or biometrics, are done at the embassy or visa centre for that country, usually in a larger city. We tell you early which steps need travel.

What does counselling cost?

Counselling with us is free. If any service fee applies later, ask for it in writing before you pay anything, and never pay a consultant for a guaranteed visa.

How do I know if Ireland is right for me?

It depends on your budget, course, English score and plans after study. Read the quick facts above, then book a session and we will compare Ireland with two or three other options for your profile.

How much does an Ireland student visa cost?

The fee is €60 for a single-entry visa and €100 for multiple entry. After arrival you also pay €300 for IRP registration.

How much money must I show?

€10,000 in accessible funds for the first year if the course is over 8 months, plus proof that at least €6,000 of tuition has been paid.
