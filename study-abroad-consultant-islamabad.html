Islamabad, and why we're upfront about not having an office there

Islamabad is Pakistan's capital and already has a dense market of study-abroad consultancies — many students there have plenty of choice. Bluezone's actual footprint is the Hazara region (Mansehra and Abbottabad) and Perth, Western Australia, with a partner office in Guangxi, China. Rather than claim a local Islamabad presence it doesn't have, this page routes Islamabad students to the nearest genuine office and explains what can be done remotely first.

If a dedicated Islamabad office opens, this page — and its structured data — will be updated to reflect it, sourced from bluezone-facts.json.

Islamabad FAQs

Does Bluezone have an office in Islamabad?

Not yet. The nearest offices are in Abbottabad and Mansehra, both reachable by road. Islamabad students can book at either, or start remotely.

Can I get guidance without traveling to Abbottabad or Mansehra?

Yes for the first consultation and much of the process; an in-person visit is most useful for document verification and pre-departure briefing.

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. The first consultation carries no obligation.

Which countries can I be guided for from Islamabad?

China, Italy, Lithuania, the United Kingdom, France and Cyprus, with further European destinations supported through our counseling desk.

Do you guarantee admission or a visa?

No. We prepare accurate, complete applications and track them to a decision. Decisions belong to universities and embassies.
